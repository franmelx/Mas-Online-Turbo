# Store listing — English

## Name

MasOnline Turbo — unofficial optimizer

## Short description

Reduces repeated requests and speeds up the MasOnline catalog without changing checkout.

## Category

Shopping

## Full description

MasOnline Turbo is an independent, unofficial extension that improves browsing on masonline.com.ar by reducing redundant catalog requests.

It batches product price and availability queries, deduplicates identical concurrent requests, and maintains short-lived caches scoped by product, region, and seller. Search, filtering, scrolling, and “Show more” become smoother while the store remains the source of truth.

Main features:

- Batches stock and price queries created by individual product cards.
- Deduplicates identical concurrent requests.
- Temporarily reuses previously requested prices, simulations, and categories.
- Automatically invalidates contextual data after region, session, or cart changes.
- Provides a local metrics and controls panel.
- Sends no information to developer-controlled servers.

The extension does not modify payments, authentication, or checkout. Cart mutations are always sent directly to the store.
